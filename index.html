Methodology & caveats

  1. Microsoft series is management-quoted capex incl. finance leases, mapped to calendar quarters (FY ends June). July 2026 lease reclassification lowers the headline ~$15B without changing underlying spend.
  2. Amazon figures are net cash capex (purchases of PP&E); gross incl. finance leases runs ~$1–2B higher per quarter.
  3. Guidance midpoints used where companies guide ranges: Alphabet $195–205B, Meta $130–145B, Micron ~$27B (FY26), TSMC $60–64B.
  4. Nvidia and Broadcom report on offset fiscal calendars; quarters are labeled fiscally with calendar coverage noted.
  5. Samsung's 2026 figure includes R&D and is from secondary reporting; SK Hynix does not disclose a single annual capex number.
  6. Mega-project figures are announced commitments, usually multi-year — not annual capex, and not directly comparable to company capex lines.
  7. Decade chart (Fig. 01): 2016–2024 are cash capex per company filings (Microsoft mapped to calendar years; Amazon net of finance leases; Meta incl. finance-lease payments from 2019). 2025 actual and 2026E use the tracker's management-quoted bases, so the 2024→2025 step includes a modest basis shift (~5–8%) alongside the real acceleration.
  8. CoreWeave quarterly series is press-release cash capex; its $35–39B FY26 guidance is on a broader committed basis (Q2 26: $9.4B committed vs $6.4B cash). Crusoe's ~$30B valuation round and Lambda's pre-IPO raise are reported/in-talks, not closed.
